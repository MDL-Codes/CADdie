# 📸 FOUND — How to Play

**Found** is a server-wide spotting game. See a fellow member out in the wild? Snap a pic, log the sighting, and rack up points.

---

## 🎯 The Goal
Earn the most points by **spotting other members IRL** (or wherever they show up). The more elusive the target, the bigger the reward.

---

## 🕹️ How to Log a Sighting

Post this in any channel:

```
!found @person
```

**You must attach a photo** as proof in the same message (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.gif`, or `.webp`).

What happens next:
1. CADdie deletes your message (so the sighting only shows up in one place).
2. The find gets reposted as an embed in the **#found** channel with your photo, the target, and the points awarded.
3. Your point total updates automatically.

---

## 💰 Points & Bounties

- Every member is worth **1 point** by default.
- Mods can put a **bounty** on someone — making them worth more points per sighting. Harder targets = bigger payday.
- Check who's currently worth extra:
  ```
  !bounties
  ```

---

## 🏆 Leaderboards

See where you stand:

```
!leaderboard
```

This shows two boards:
- 🏆 **Finders Leaderboard** — most points earned
- 👁️ **Most Spotted Leaderboard** — most times found

---

## 🛠️ Mod Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `!setbounty @person <points>` | Set a custom bounty value on someone |
| `!removebounty @person` | Reset their bounty back to 1 pt |

---

## ✅ Quick Rules Recap

- 📸 **Photo required** — no pic, no points.
- 🏷️ **@mention the person** you found.
- 🤝 **Be a good sport** — keep sightings fun and consensual.
- 🆘 Type `!foundhelp` any time to see these rules in Discord.

Happy hunting! 🕵️
